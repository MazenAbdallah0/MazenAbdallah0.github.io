01Power BI

Olist E-Commerce Sales & Performance Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard that turns raw Olist transactional data into commercial, logistics and customer-satisfaction insights, built on multi-table data modeling.

$13.59MTotal revenue
99KOrders
89.15%On-time delivery
12.5Avg delivery (days)

Key insights

  • Total revenue of $13.59M across 99K orders, with $2.25M in freight and an average review score of 4.09.
  • São Paulo (SP) is the top revenue state at $5.2M, followed by RJ ($1.8M) and MG ($1.6M).
  • Average delivery time is 12.50 days, with an 89.15% on-time rate and 7,827 late orders.
  • The longest average delivery times are in RR (29.3 days), AP (27.2) and AM (26.4).
  • Power BI Desktop
  • Power Query
  • DAX
  • Data Modeling
  • Star Schema
View on GitHub
Case study details
Business problem
Evaluate sales trends, regional revenue, payment methods and product categories; track delivery performance and late shipments; and see how delivery delays relate to customer review scores.
Dataset
Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset: orders, order items, payments, reviews, customers, products, sellers and geolocation tables.
Analysis process
  1. Cleaned and transformed the transactional tables in Power Query, including null-value clean-ups.
  2. Modeled the data as a star schema with three fact tables (order items, payments, reviews) and five dimensions (orders, customers, products, sellers, geolocation).
  3. Wrote DAX measures for total revenue, orders, freight, average review score, average delivery days, on-time rate and late orders.
  4. Designed a dark-themed, two-page dashboard (Sales & Revenue; Operations & Logistics) with year and status slicers.

Superstore Executive BI Sales Dashboard

A high-end, dark-themed Power BI executive dashboard tracking retail sales performance, profit margins, total quantities and regional KPIs using custom DAX measures.

Case study details
Business problem
Give decision-makers a single view of how sales, profit, order volume and discounts perform across regions, sub-categories and years.

Superstore retail sales data with orders, regions, categories and sub-categories, covering 2014 to 2017.

03Python

EDA: Wine Quality Project

Exploratory data analysis in Python on a wine quality dataset: cleaning, visualizing and interpreting the data to uncover the key factors affecting wine ratings.

1,599Samples
240Duplicates removed
+0.48Alcohol–quality correlation
−0.40Volatile acidity–quality correlation

Key insights

  • Alcohol has the strongest positive correlation with quality (0.48): wines with higher alcohol tend to be rated higher.
  • Volatile acidity is negatively correlated with quality (-0.40).
  • Most samples are rated 5 or 6, and very high or very low ratings are rare, so the classes are slightly imbalanced, which matters before training a classifier.
  • No single feature dominates quality; the other correlations are weak to moderate.
  • Python
  • Pandas
  • NumPy
  • Matplotlib
  • Seaborn
  • Jupyter Notebook
  • Git
View on GitHub
Case study details
Business problem
Which physicochemical properties are associated with higher or lower wine quality, and is the data clean and balanced enough for later modeling?
Dataset
Red wine physicochemical dataset (winequality-red.csv): 1,599 samples and 12 numeric columns, with quality as the target variable.
Analysis process
  1. Loaded the dataset and inspected its structure, data types and descriptive statistics.
  2. Checked for missing values (none found) and detected 240 duplicate rows.
  3. Removed the duplicates, leaving 1,359 rows.
  4. Explored correlations with a heatmap, the quality distribution, histograms, pair plots, box plots and scatter plots.
  5. Documented observations and a final conclusion in the notebook.
